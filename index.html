About this project

Why map these journeys?

She was there. But where she stood mattered.

This digital object compares Krishnabhabini Das’s 1882 journey with Durgabati Ghose’s 1932 journey. It treats travel not simply as a series of destinations but as a changing position from which the traveller observes, compares, interprets and is herself observed.

The basic unit is a travel episode: a meaningful moment in which movement, place, encounter and interpretation come together. The map is therefore a form of spatial close reading rather than an exhaustive reconstruction of every movement.

Method

Spatial close reading, not exhaustive GIS

The map does not treat every place-name as equivalent. Episodes are selected when physical movement or location changes the act of looking, the traveller's self-positioning, or the interpretation of people, institutions, landscapes and infrastructures.

Travelling gaze is used here descriptively: the traveller's interpretive position changes as her body moves through space. She may look outward, compare home and abroad, be looked at by others, or be classified by institutions.

Route before pin

Transit, arrival, residence and return are preserved as different stages of travel.

Uncertainty stays visible

City-level, district-level and approximate historical locations are labelled rather than given false precision.

Gaze is relational

The traveller may look outward, compare home and abroad, look at herself, be looked at by others, or be classified by institutions.

Sources

Primary travel corpus

A Bengali Lady in England by Krishnabhabini Das and The Westward Traveller by Durgabati Ghose form the principal mapped corpus. The interface uses short source locators and analytical summaries; final thesis citations remain authoritative.

Rassundari Devi and Binodini Dasi remain comparative life-writing texts in the written thesis, but are not mapped as equivalent travel journeys here. A few return-route details remain marked for final primary-text verification before submission/public hosting.
